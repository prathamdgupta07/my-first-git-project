How does AI help cybersecurity teams?

AI can scan large volumes of digital activity and highlight patterns that may require a security expert’s attention.

What is AI in cybersecurity?

AI in cybersecurity uses patterns in network activity, messages, and accounts to help detect possible fraud, phishing, or unusual behaviour.

How does AI detect cyber threats?

A system can compare current activity with normal behaviour and flag unusual events. This helps teams sort through alerts and focus their time on higher-risk cases.

Quick answer: AI can speed up threat detection, but security professionals investigate and decide what action to take.

Can AI create new security risks?

Yes. Attackers can also use AI to create convincing scams or automate attacks. Good security combines technology with strong passwords, updates, training, and careful human checks.
