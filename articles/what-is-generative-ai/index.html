Generative AI · 6 minute read

What is generative AI and what can it create?

Generative AI produces new content from patterns learned from large collections of examples.

What is generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of AI that can create text, images, audio, video, or computer code. It predicts likely next pieces of content based on patterns it learned during training.

How do generative AI tools create content?

A user gives a prompt, such as a question or instruction. The tool uses its learned patterns to build a response that fits the request. It does not simply copy one source; it generates a new output based on statistical relationships.

Quick answer: Generative AI is a creative assistant, but it is not an automatic source of truth.

Why should people review AI content?

Generated work can contain errors, biased wording, missing context, or made-up details. Students should verify facts, cite real sources, and add their own judgement before using it in school or work.
