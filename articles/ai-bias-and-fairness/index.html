What are AI bias and fairness?

AI systems can produce unequal results when the data, assumptions, or goals behind them do not represent people fairly.

What is AI bias?

AI bias happens when a system’s output unfairly favours or disadvantages certain people or groups. It can enter through unbalanced data, historical inequalities, narrow testing, or design choices.

Why is fairness difficult for AI?

Fairness has more than one meaning. A useful system must be tested with the people it affects, measured for different outcomes, and reviewed when its context changes.

Quick answer: Fair AI is not created once; it is checked, improved, and governed over time.

How can teams reduce AI bias?

Teams can use diverse data, test across groups, document their choices, invite independent review, and give people a way to challenge high-impact decisions.
