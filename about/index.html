About the project

An AI blog designed for learning, not showing off.

After Tomorrow turns large technology topics into short, readable guides for students. It pairs visual design with useful information and responsible questions.

What makes this blog easy to use?

Each page has one clear topic, a descriptive URL, a unique title and meta description, labelled images, and useful links. The articles also use question-and-answer sections where a direct explanation is most helpful.
